# What's new in 2026?

## ✈️ SIIM26 was in Pittsburgh, PA (June 10-12)
The annual meeting was in Pittsburgh, PA from June 10-12. As always, the hackathon ran in hybrid format, so you could take part fully virtually or join us in person.

Pathologists and imagers could also use the SIIM-DPA Bridge Pass, which gave access to both SIIM26 and PathVisions26.


## 📝️ A [Registration Form](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/55e079d9c30c40deb4ae7b6805419804) <!-- guardrails: allow -->
Why a registration form?!? SIIM Hackathons have always been a casual experience and it remains so. However, you are highly encouraged to use this [registration form](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/55e079d9c30c40deb4ae7b6805419804) so we can reliably share announcements and updates. Help us help you get the most out of your SIIM Hackathon experience! <!-- guardrails: allow -->


## 💡️ A New Idea Pitch Form
The freedom to pick and choose your projects that you know and love at the SIIM hackathon will always be there.

But for those who prefer a more structured approach, or want to browse ideas currently being mulled for this year's hackathon, we have an [idea pitch form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScZPLy4obTYKS30FEWGDH-9FfzG2w4WJkKVZWbUZjHEf1Mjmg/viewform?usp=publish-editor) where you can suggest ideas and projects to tackle this year and nominate a team lead (yourself or someone else).


## 🧬️ Synthea Patients on Both FHIR Servers
As of November 2026, the R5 and R4 FHIR servers each have 40 synthetic patients made with [Synthea](https://synthetichealth.github.io/synthea/), on top of the 10 SIIM demo patients. Each one has about 10 years of history. See [Hackathon Event](../getting-started/hackathon-event.md) for how to find them.


## 🤖️ A Guide for AI Coding Agents
For the RSNA 2026 Vibe Coding Hackathon we added a [guide to using AI coding agents](../getting-started/ai-agents.md) with the server, plus instruction files you can drop into your project for Claude Code, Codex, Copilot and Kiro.
